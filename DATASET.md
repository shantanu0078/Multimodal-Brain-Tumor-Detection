# Dataset

## Overview

The proposed multimodal brain tumor detection framework was developed and evaluated on **two publicly available CT–MRI brain tumor imaging datasets** obtained from Kaggle. Each dataset was used **independently** for model training and evaluation to assess the robustness and generalization capability of the proposed framework across different data distributions.

The datasets are **not included** in this repository due to their respective licensing terms. Please download them directly from the original sources before reproducing the experiments.

## Dataset Characteristics

Both datasets provide:

* **Modalities:** Computed Tomography (CT) and Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI)
* **Task:** Brain Tumor Classification
* **Classes:** Tumor and Healthy
* **Evaluation Protocol:** Each dataset was trained and evaluated separately without combining the datasets.
* **Data Source:** Publicly available Kaggle datasets
